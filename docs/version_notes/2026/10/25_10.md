## ℹ️ FYI — ci-svc is planned to live in this repo

The charter's `ci-svc` (reads `.git2-ci.yml`, plans pipelines, queues jobs,
keeps logs and artifacts, stores CI secrets) is planned to be built here, next
to the runner, under the same name. The git2 repo would then have no `ci-svc/`
directory. See [plan.md §2](../../../design/plan.md#2-where-the-project-documents-disagree)
for why.

What that means for you:

- You call ci-svc over gRPC (package `ci.v1`), not a REST API.
- Its tables live in the `ci_svc` schema. Like everyone's, the migrations go
  into git2 by PR.
- Its events (`ci.v1.PipelineFinished` and others) go through the same
  Postgres outbox as everyone else's.
