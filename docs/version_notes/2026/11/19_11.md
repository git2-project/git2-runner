**Audience:** Console (Beste), Deploy (Ceyda), Dash (Bilge) and their coding agents.
**Scope:** Monitoring for the console. Runners now check their Docker host and sample their job containers, and three admin calls show it: `Runner.health`, `ListRunningJobs` and `GetOverview`. Migration 008 stores the latest reports.

## 🟢 NEW — runner and container monitoring (`RunnerAdminService`)

```
runner ── RequestJob + RunnerHealth ─────────► ci-svc ── runners.health
       ── UpdateJob RUNNING + ContainerStats ─►        ── jobs.container_stats
console ◄─ admin-api ◄─ GetRunner · ListRunningJobs · GetOverview
```

| Call | Gives the console |
|---|---|
| `GetRunner`, `ListRunners` → `Runner.health` | Docker reachable or not, version, platform, CPUs, memory, the runner's job containers (leftovers show here), `reported_at` |
| `ListRunningJobs` (optional `runner_id`) | each running job with its pipeline, runner, `started_at` and its container's CPU %, memory and processes against their limits |
| `GetOverview` | runners online, offline, paused and **with Docker down**; jobs pending and running; the oldest waiting job; the last 24 h of pipelines by status |

- Runners send the check with every `RequestJob` (at most every 25 s) and a container sample with every 15 s heartbeat. The console polls; every 15–30 s is enough.
- `cpu_percent` reads like `docker stats`: 100 is one full CPU.
- An online runner whose Docker does not answer still takes jobs and fails them. That is the first thing to show in red.
